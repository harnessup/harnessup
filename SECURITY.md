# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **support@harnessup.dev** with the subject **Security**.

Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability: the report would be visible to everyone before a fix exists.

## What to include

- a description of the problem and the impact you expect;
- the HarnessUp version (output of `harnessup --version`) and your operating system;
- steps to reproduce;
- a proof of concept, if you have one;
- how you would like to be credited, if at all.

Do not include real keys, tokens or personal data in the report.

## What to expect

We aim to reply within a few business days. We do not promise a fixed deadline for the fix; we will keep you posted as the investigation moves forward.

## Scope

This policy covers the HarnessUp product and the installers distributed through this repository and the official website.

## Coordinated disclosure

Please do not disclose the problem publicly until a fix is available or until we agree on a date together. Once the fix ships, the reporter is credited if they wish.
